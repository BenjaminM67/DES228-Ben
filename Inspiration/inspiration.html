In creating this page, I learned the alt text attribute when I was wondering how it could be incorporated into my code without adding extra content and further congesting my page with word people will probably skip over anyway. As someone responded to my post in a previous assignment, I noticed all my inspirations are people of color and it honestly was never my intention to solely choose from among individuals under that umbrella but looking back on my upbringing it only makes sense. I modified my alt text further by shortening it to the barebones of what it is to further move away from biased terminology that would only muddle the understanding for people who rely on it for pure information only. With the paths I found it easier to delete all the folders I didn’t need and doing that fixed my issue of the css breaking when I put the html in the root folder which honestly has frustrated me to no end. I was playing around with the orientation of my descriptions and images and while doing that I found it more appealing to introduce visitors to the photo and have it lead into the description below them and kept it uniform for all of the images that followed.
